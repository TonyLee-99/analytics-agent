Dataset: Performance Metrics - Business Affairs & Consumer Protection - Limited Business Licenses
Portal ID: 7483-qiqw
Category: Administration & Finance
Attribution: City of Chicago
Rows last updated: 2011-10-19

Description:
Limited Business Licenses (LBLs) are required for all Chicago businesses not specifically exempt from licensing requirements. This includes all business that provide services, or engage in office operations or general sales. Currently the performance target for processing Limited Business Licenses is 3 days.

Columns:
- Week (text)
- Total Licenses Issued (number)
- Average Days to Issue License (number)
- Median Days to Issue License (number)
- Target Days to Issue License (number)
- Quarter (text)
- Weeks Target Was Achieved (number)
- Weeks Target Was Not Achieved (number)
