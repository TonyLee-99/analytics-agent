Dataset: Performance Metrics - Housing & Economic Development - Affordable Multi Family Homes
Portal ID: dsvs-yfj6
Category: Administration & Finance
Attribution: City of Chicago
Rows last updated: 2012-09-19

Description:
Multi-family programs that support the creation of new affordable multi-family housing units or the preservation of existing affordable units. Units are counted when financial commitments are approved by the City Council.

Columns:
- Month (text)
- Year (text)
- Created/Preserved Multi-Family Units (number)
- Target Homes (number)
- Progress to Target Homes (number)
- Total Spending (number)
- Spending Target (number)
