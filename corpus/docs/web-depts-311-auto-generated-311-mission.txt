## Mission
The mission of the 311 City Services Call Center is to:
Serve as the point of entry for residents, business owners and visitors that need easy access to information regarding City programs, services and events.
Record and efficiently document all requests for non-emergency City services within the Customer Relationship Management (CRM) System and forward them to the proper governmental and non-governmental agencies.
Assist City departments, governmental and non-governmental agencies deliver improved customer service and manage resources more efficiently.
Monitor and provide consistent, essential performance management reports and analysis of City services delivery.
Direct requests for non-emergency police reports to the Alternate Response Section (ARS).
Serve as the back-up facility for 911.
