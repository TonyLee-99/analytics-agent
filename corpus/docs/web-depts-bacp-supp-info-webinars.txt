## Free Business Education Webinars
The Chicago Department of Business Affairs and Consumer Protection (BACP) offers free business education webinars every Wednesday afternoon and Friday morning. Topics include business licensing, operations, financial resources, marketing, and more. Programs are free, hosted online and open to the public and taught by industry professionals, not-for-profit agencies, and government agencies.
BACP is committed to providing accessibility for all individuals. To request accessibility, please email BACPoutreach@cityofchicago.org or call 312-744-6060. BACP will always work to fulfill accessibility needs. In order to ensure the best quality service is provided, please make requests at least 5 business days in advance.
By registering for these webinars, you consent to receive emails regarding the webinar/s as well as other BACP communications. Your information may be shared with webinar presenters/speakers who provide follow-up materials and resources. You can unsubscribe from these emails at any time.
