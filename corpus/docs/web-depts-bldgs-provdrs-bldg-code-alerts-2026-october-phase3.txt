## Implementation of Phase 3 Construction Codes Modernization Ordinance
## October 6, 2026
Phase 3 of the Chicago Construction Codes Modernization Ordinance is now available for implementation!
As of October 1, 2026, permit applicants may elect to use the new 2026 Chicago Fuel Gas, Mechanical, and Energy Transformation Codes, along with amendments to the 2019 Building Code, Building Rehabilitation Code, and Administrative Provisions, to help build more homes in Chicago.
The new provisions are mandatory for permit applications started on or after December 1, 2026 .
## Applications submitted before October 1
Applications already in review may elect to use the new provisions by notifying their assigned Project Manager by email. An additional round of plan review may be required.
For full transition requirements, including drawing labels and exceptions, read the Commissioner’s transition memo . Questions can be sent to DOBCommissioner@cityofchicago.org .
