## 2026 Structural Peer Review Training Class
## October 28, 2026
The Department of Buildings is pleased to announce that the structural peer reviewer training class for 2026 will be held December 1, 2026, at Harold Washington College, 30 East Lake Street.
Successful completion of the half-day training class is a requirement for registration as a registered structural peer reviewer.
Application and Registration
## To Apply
The application for the 2026 training class opens October 28, 2026, and will close at 4:00 p.m. on Monday, November 23, 2026 .
Apply Now
## Application Review
Applications for the 2026 training class will be reviewed on a rolling basis.
Completing the application does not guarantee your enrollment in the 2026 training class.
If the Department of Buildings requires additional information to process your application, you will be contacted by email. Please respond to follow-up requests promptly.
## Capacity
The Department of Buildings has planned this event based on our best estimates of demand for participation. In past years, however, training classes have reached capacity before the close of the application period.
Registration
Admitted applicants will be notified by email and provided with instructions to pay the registration fee in person at a City of Chicago payment center. The Department anticipates it will begin sending notifications and payment instructions within 3 business days after the application is received. Payment will be due before the class begins on December 1, 2026.
Program Details
## Overview
The half-day structural peer reviewer training class provides an intensive overview of important requirements of Chicago’s Registered Structural Peer Reviewer Program and other related topics.
## Who Should Attend?
The training class is for both first time and renewing registered structural peer reviewers.
If there are available slots, other industry professionals may be approved to audit the training class.
## Schedule
Doors open and check-in will begin at 8:00 a.m. and the program will begin at 9:00 a.m.
The program is scheduled to end by 12:45 p.m.
## Attendance
Attendees will be required to sign in and sign out.
Participants who miss more than 15 minutes of the program will not be considered to have successfully completed the training class and will not receive continuing education credit.
## Participation
Attendees are expected to be fully engaged in the training class and should not plan to multi-task during scheduled class time.
## Meals
A continental breakfast, including coffee and tea, will be provided.
## Continuing Education Credit
Participants who attend the full training class are eligible for 3 hours of continuing education credit. The Department of Buildings works with the International Code Council (ICC) to track and report continuing education credit.
## Classroom
Free wireless internet service is available in the room at Harold Washington College. Attendees will be seated at round tables.
Please note that only a very limited number of electrical outlets are available. Please do not plan to be able to charge device(s).
## Training Materials
All attendees will receive a print copy of the Chicago Plan Review Manual Volume III: Structural Plan Review (2026 edition).

For a registration cancelled after November 16, 2026, any refund or future-year credit is subject to the cancellation fee.

## Information for First Time Registrants
Individuals who have been licensed to practice structural engineering in Illinois for at least three years and been licensed by any jurisdiction to practice structural engineering, civil engineering, professional engineering or architecture for at least 6 years, have acted as either professional of record or project lead, and /or performed a structural peer review on at least five City of Chicago plan-based building permits issued on or after January 1, 2020, and meet other requirements set forth in the Rules for the Structural Peer Review Program are eligible to apply for the training class to register as registered structural peer reviewers.
Please note that the terms “professional of record” and “project lead” are defined in the Rules.
## Expiration Date
First time registrants who successfully complete the 2026 training class and other requirements for registration as a self-certification professional will be registered with an expiration date of July 31, 2032.
## Information for Renewing RSPRs
The 2026 training class is required for registered structural peer reviewers with registrations that are scheduled to expire December 31, 2026 (automatically extended from July 31, 2026), if they wish to continue in the program.
Following successful completion of the 2026 training class, these registrations will be renewed to July 31, 2032.
