Contacting CACC:
AskCACC@cityofchicago.org : This is our general inquiry email, including requests to speak at Commission Meetings, temporary
animal exhibit permits, and other broad inquiries not addressed on our website (not for questions related to specific animals).
CACCRescue@cityofchicago.org : This email is for use by our approved Homeward Bound partners or those interested in becoming rescue partners.
CACCVolunteer@cityofchicago.org : This email is for all volunteer-related communication, including inquiries from current, new, and potential volunteers,
as well as group volunteer opportunities.
CACCfoster@cityofchicago.org : This email is dedicated to inquiries about fostering, including our Doggie Day Out program.
Note: VisitCACC: This email was used during the pandemic for intake appointment scheduling but is no longer active. With the return to in-person operations, appointments are no longer required.
If you are looking for your lost pet or are interested in adopting any animal, you must visit CACC in person between the hours of 12p.m. and 7 p.m. daily.
