## Major Projects, Programs and Studies
In addition to the everyday neighborhood services and improvements CDOT delivers across Chicago, the Department also manages major capital projects that are essential to keeping the city moving. These large-scale efforts include projects like bridge and viaduct rehabilitations and major corridor reconstructions. They require years of planning, extensive coordination with partners, and significant investment. Together, they ensure Chicago’s infrastructure remains safe, reliable, and resilient for generations to come.
