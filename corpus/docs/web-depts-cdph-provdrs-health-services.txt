## Clinical Services
CDPH operates Immunization Clinics, Mental Health Centers, and STI Specialty clinics to provide a variety of primary and preventative care services to support underserved populations across Chicago. CDPH partners with community mental health providers and invests in local agencies to ensure all residents have access to mental health services. These services are provided free of charge to all Chicagoans who are unable to pay. CDPH STI specialty clinics diagnose and treat common sexually-transmitted infections and provide partner services as well.
Learn more in our full listing of Clinical Services pages .
## Accessibility and Accommodations
CDPH is committed to creating an accessible environment for all visitors.
To request an accommodation, look for these signs at our community clinics and please speak with a staff member for assistance. You may submit a request online via our Accessibility Request Form .
