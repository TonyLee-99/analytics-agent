## HIV, AIDS, and STIs
The CDPH Syndemic Infectious Disease (SID) Bureau monitors HIV and STIs to understand transmission patterns within the city of Chicago. CDPH works in partnership with the communities we serve, to improve STI prevention, treatment and care, and promote the health and well-being of those affected by STIs, HIV, and AIDS. As Chicagoans often face challenges to accessing and utilizing healthcare, we also invest significant resources in programs that provide supportive services such as housing, transportation, behavioral health, and emergency financial assistance. Our Sexual Health Specialty Clinics offer a safe, welcoming, and confidential environment where patients can receive high-quality testing, treatment, and resources for HIV and sexually transmitted infections.
