## Infectious Disease Prevention
CDPH's Infectious Disease Bureau is dedicated to protecting the health and well-being of Chicagoans by preventing, monitoring, and responding to infectious diseases. Guided by principles of health equity, cultural responsiveness, and trauma-informed care, our mission is to ensure that all individuals and communities have access to critical resources, information, and services needed to prevent and manage infectious diseases effectively.
Throughout this website, we provide a wealth of information about our diverse programs and initiatives. Explore resources about infectious diseases, access data dashboards, learn about vaccines , and stay informed about emerging health threats. CDPH is here to serve as a trusted partner in safeguarding public health; together, we are building a healthier Chicago.
