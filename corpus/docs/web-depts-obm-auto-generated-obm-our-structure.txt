## Our Structure
CAPITAL PLANNING AND GRANTS MANAGEMENT: Prepares the annual grant and capital budget recommendations and the City’s five-year capital improvement plan, and monitors capital and grant expenditures and performance.
BUDGET DEVELOPMENT AND MONITORING: Prepares annual budget forecasts and recommendations, and assists departments in the management of their budgets throughout the year.
WORKFORCE MANAGEMENT AND TECHNOLOGY: Leverages data to create innovative management solutions and identify process efficiencies. Advances and supports citywide operating and capital budget technology systems. Oversees enterprise management of personnel compensation, workforce management and performance initiatives.
STRATEGIC COMMUNICATIONS: Manages legislative analysis and Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests. Liaises with members of City Council, press, and the community, and leads the development of OBM's major publications and communications.
