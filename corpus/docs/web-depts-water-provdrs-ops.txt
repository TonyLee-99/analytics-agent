## Operations & Distribution
The Chicago Department of Water Management's Bureau of Operations and Distribution's primary responsibility is to maintain the water and sewer system. This includes 4,300 miles of water mains, 4,500 miles of sewer mains, 47,000 fire hydrants, 48,000 water control valves, and 56,000 sewer structures.
